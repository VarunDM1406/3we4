Privacy policy

Last updated 21 September 2026. Plain-language summary first, detail below.

Who we are

3WE4.media is a digital growth agency based in Greater Noida, India. This policy explains what personal information this website handles. You can reach us at 3WE4.media@gmail.com.

What we collect

This website has no accounts, no payments and no contact forms that store data. We only receive what you choose to send us by email, for example your name, your business and what you want help with.

The "notify me" box in the footer opens your own email app. Nothing is sent to us until you send that email yourself.

Cookies and similar storage

We store one small item in your browser (local storage) to remember your cookie choice. It contains no personal information.

Analytics, if we switch it on, only runs after you press Accept on the cookie banner. If you decline, it does not load. You can change your choice at any time using "Cookie settings" in the footer.

Third parties

Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which means your browser contacts Google servers and your IP address is visible to them. Our hosting provider also keeps standard server logs, such as IP address and pages requested, for security and reliability.

Some pages link to outside sources (for example DataReportal and PwC). Those sites have their own privacy policies.

How we use your information

We use what you email us to reply to your enquiry, prepare a quote and run the work if you become a client. We do not sell your information.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry emails for as long as they are needed for that conversation or working relationship, and for a reasonable period after for our records.

Your rights

You can ask us what information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it, in line with applicable Indian law, including the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023. Email us and we will respond.

Children

This website is for businesses and is not directed at children.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top of this page.
