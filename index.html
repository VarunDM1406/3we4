The mindset

Liability or asset.
You're running one of them.

A liability is something you pay for and get nothing back. An asset is something you build once that keeps working for you. Social media is neither by nature — how you run it decides which one you end up with.

Run as a liability

Posted when someone remembers. Judged by likes.

Handed to whoever is free. No goal behind any post. Nobody can say what it has earned.

So it earns nothing — and that gets read as proof that social media doesn't work.

VS

Run as an asset

Every post has a job: get found, earn trust, or drive a sale.

One accountable owner. Strategy first, then production, then distribution.

Measured in leads and sales — so you can see exactly what it has earned.

What it costs you

A weak presence sends no invoice.
It just costs you sales.

A business can lose a sale at three different points between a stranger and a customer — and never see it happen, because nobody bills you for a sale you didn't get.

01 Leak 01

They never find you

Your customer searches, scrolls, asks a friend. If you aren't where they look, you don't lose the sale — you never even learn it existed.

02 Leak 02

They find you, then leave

A page that has gone quiet, weak visuals, no proof. They don't decide you're bad. They decide someone else looks safer.

03 Leak 03

They trust you. Nothing happens.

No clear next step, no follow-through, no funnel. Attention arrives and leaves without ever becoming a customer.
